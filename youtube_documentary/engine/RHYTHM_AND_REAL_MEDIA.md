# Rhythm and truthful media — reusable editorial lessons

These are judgement aids, not a mandatory montage/template or an artistic certificate.

1. Faster editing is not global speech acceleration. Preserve a chosen natural voice/performance; remove verified quiet pauses or explicitly selected filler with an exposed source/output map and tiny boundary crossfades. Never delete meaningful words automatically.
2. A larger cut count can still feel static if neighboring shots repeat nearly identical crops of one source. Inspect the encoded sequence, then change source/meaning or keep an honestly motivated longer hold. Do not count invisible crop changes as variety.
3. Intercut source statements because they contribute information. Keep each source's actual meaning/speaker/date. Two voices from one interview are NOT two news broadcasts. A blocked source is not permission to fabricate one.
4. Source words must actually be readable. Inspect native glyph pixels, not just the requested crop dimensions. Do not enlarge7pxletters then hide their failure with rapid cuts/blur. Match ink anchors/static scale when the word is the motif; hold long enough for its job.2seconds is this film's decision, not every future film's fixed rule.
5. Marker emphasis is editorial, never part of the alleged historical evidence. Multiplication can preserve original black ink. Do not retype archival words and claim they are source pixels.
6. A historical print scan and a current webpage rendering are different material types (`press_scan` / `press_webpage`). Both may be useful when accurately credited, neither establishes publication rights.
7. A visible h1DOMbox does NOT prove its screenshot is unobscured. Cookie-consent prose can cover the source word. Inspect the actual saved image. Use only normal public browsing choices; never remove access overlays or bypass login/subscription blocks to invent a clean source.
8. Acquire more than you use. Reject sources that add softness, unsupported identity, foreign-language slogan, irrelevant subject or filler rather than substance. A genuine but unusable candidate belongs in research, not the film ingredient list.
9. Exact-subject housing is better evidence for housing than a generic city. A contemporary city must not masquerade as1980sfootage; a photo with editorial depth must not masquerade as recorded subject motion.
10. Review the actual final encode at every0.5seconds, all joins and meaningful subsecond phases. Full decode/levels/ASR supplement frame inspection. Say exactly what was inspected: sampled images are not all source frames, ASR is not human listening, and tests are not creative/user approval.

## Revision5 corrections — source identity and performance

- An ASR quote/time is not proof that its speaker is on screen. The promising56-ton Mermelstein quote plays over empty seats; it was rejected for a prompt face reveal. The selected on-camera threat gives this edit a real firsthand stake. Do not paste another speaking moment over it.
- Count programmes, interview contexts and people separately. Revision5's two expert/insider interviews share FRONTLINE; BBC/AJ supply dated moving imagery, not claimed extra English interview voices.
- Native programmes may internally reuse footage. Distinct programme timestamps are not automatically distinct visual evidence. No still is reused in the new cut; native prologue/night-street reuse is not claimed as new independent footage.
- Use new requested same-identity takes; retain raw recordings. Contrast the energetic opening/question with a calmer numerical explanation. WPM, ASR and silence edits cannot certify emotion/naturalness or override feedback.
- Quiet-pause targets must preserve the70ms word guards on BOTH sides. A target below140ms now raises an error instead of silently failing to shorten gaps. Nothing here authorizes time-stretching archive speech.
