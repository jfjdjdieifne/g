## Chosen premise
A human can cross a quiet supermassive black hole’s point of no return alive, without a sudden impact. Why does a friend outside see a different end? Why can even an outward beam not escape? A single astronaut, a consistent distant ship, a beacon, and an increasingly revealing cutaway carry the explanation. Not an archive montage or a lecture list.

## Story and visual escalation
1. Cold open beside the falling astronaut: no wall, then an unanswered message; reveal the enormous black hole.
2. Scale comparison changes the tidal gradient, not merely sphere size. Return to the same supermassive destination.
3. Beacon crosses the gap to an actual distant receiver; arrivals spread, change color, fade. Do not show an eternally visible frozen statue.
4. Enter the astronaut’s frame: normal watch; cross the schematic horizon without collision. Camera moves to the radial escape diagram.
5. Explicit schematic of outward/sideways/inward light paths on a nonrotating model; all inside stay inside. Show WHY a larger engine is not the solution.
6. Tidal deformation deeper in; no gore, portal, or invented photograph of the singularity. End returns to the first calm horizon crossing, now understood.
