# Current user rules — all film styles

Effective 2026-10-16. This is the current creative policy; old numbered guides, YAML v2, demos and old film instructions are historical if they conflict. A later explicit user instruction takes precedence; record it here with its scope instead of silently adding another rival handbook. Do not turn one film's treatment into a permanent universal formula.

## Assignment, language and architecture

- Speak briefly in Levantine/Syrian Arabic to the user, who uses an iPhone. Explain unfamiliar terms simply.
- **All new narration and authored video display are English.** Arabic is for conversation/internal notes, not automatic captions.
- Current assignment is organization/onboarding **before more video changes**. No unsolicited topic, film, render, voice replacement or new production authorization follows from opening this repository.
- One shared engine and tools, with per-film profiles. No copied engine per subject or style.
- Choose the explicitly requested style. If materially unspecified, propose/ask one concise style decision, not the whole already-established preference list. No fixed medium percentages, shot counts, asset quotas, duration or forced template. Historic 4–9-minute scope is not a universal duration requirement.
- Topic should have curiosity, stakes and an earned payoff. A polished weak topic was rejected. Never promise views.

## Voice and writing

- **Permanently approved default: voice03 identity AND native clean extended performance.** Preserve the exact WAV/reference hash in REFERENCE_INDEX and DEFAULT_VOICE. Historical films remain untouched.
- A local `voice-03` number is not a universal voice/model ID. A WAV is a reference, not an executable model. Restore/resolve the actual approved registration before new synthesis; do not assume a different session's number is identical. Do not re-audition, invent a provider name, or silently substitute. If technically unavailable, disclose that blocker; do not mask it with music or speed changes.
- The four replacement voice02 Titan takes were rejected as robotic. Old approved Escobar processing is historical permission, not permission to process a new robotic performance into approval.
- Write like a knowledgeable, personable YouTuber explaining a connected idea. Natural links, varied emphasis and native pace; not a fact chain, grand dramatic sentence parade, serial rhetorical questions, fake hesitation, artificial breaths or falsely claimed human recording.
- Earlier turtle-slow narration was rejected twice. Keep the approved native performance; occasional short meaningful pauses or emphasis on 2–3 important words are not a license for uniformly slow delivery. Do not accelerate or pitch-shift new narration to conceal poor acting.
- Preserve scripts, untouched raw takes, their provenance/hashes and derived edits separately.

## Visual storytelling and explanation — applies to EVERY style

- Visually exciting, professional, **meaningful motion and camera direction should dominate the film**. Motivated readable holds are allowed; motion is not compulsory every second. No static slideshow, purposeless 2D, or an isolated 3D object on PowerPoint.
- Explain difficult ideas causally and proactively: what acts on what, why it changes, and what follows. A model plus technical labels is not explanation. Show informative side/cut faces and transformations; do not orbit an irrelevant rear surface.
- Choose the BEST real footage, photo, motion, map, graphic or model for the specific idea and selected style; connect them into ONE story. In archive-dependent styles, inspect exact-subject genuine material before deciding to manufacture replacements. Pure explanation need not display archive.
- No ordinary cut-cut photo chain. Use motivated camera travel, handoffs, overlaps, interacting layers and 2–3 scenes in a connected space where appropriate. Do not impose one board/zoom/preset transition on everything.
- Real source motion, readable staging and causal change matter more than textures, stamps, shadows, grain or arbitrary orbiting. No orange rectangles/Paint arrows/chips as a substitute for direction. A marker/pin/token zoom alone is not a meaningful map explanation.
- Accurate maps are an independent cross-style requirement: truthful geography, positions, routes, scale, sources and movement that explains. No irrelevant map in Titan. Use robust fonts, no missing glyphs.
- Recurring white full-paragraph source screenshots were rejected as Facebook-post-like. Integrate evidence with relevant original words, cropped/contextualized appropriately; a source page is not automatically a finished shot. Do not fabricate quoted words or sources.
- No blind asset reuse. A motivated return is allowed; repeated crops do not count as fresh material. Enough sources for the story, not a quota or indiscriminate batch download. Multiple meaningful shots per spoken sentence can help. An exciting opening may slow down only for meaning.
- Meaningful text only; captions OFF by default. One text layer per sentence; no duplicate word-motion and subtitles. Prior phone-readability guidance: a push on more than four words should remain within about 10%, not destroy reading.

## Sound and evidence

- Contextual visual AND sound effects, with motivated bridges and pauses; no generic preset noise. Historical SFX direction kept effects below music (roughly −7 dB relative), a mix reference rather than universal loudness calibration. Do not import an old blanket no-music policy into a different authorized film; sound choices must fit the actual brief.
- A source speaker's authentic sound before revealing/reframing the person was liked. It is an available technique, not a mandatory news→narrator formula. Static name/shutter/marker motifs and an audio-only presenter hidden behind unrelated images were rejected.
- Real footage/photo/press first for documentary evidence. Generated historical people, anchors, interviews, quotes, news reports, signs or records must never masquerade as genuine evidence. Inspect genuine references before derived historical imagery and disclose illustration/unknown areas.
- Track source, author, date, exact subject/event, acquisition, transformations, usage and rights. Attribution, a government-hosted PDF or a public-domain label does not clear third-party OceanGate/AP/Pelagic/Garry Comber material. Do not declare publication rights without evidence.
- User allowed online downloaders/GitHub; declined cookies. Do not request passwords, tokens, cookies or 2FA in chat.

## Actual review and approval

- Inspect **each actual encoded scene during production**, repair clarity, beauty, causal explanation, motion, continuity and SFX **before full assembly**. Do not discover fundamental visual failures only after a full render or make the user diagnose unseen work.
- Review chronological 0.5-second/2fps samples without skips, with finer subsecond checks for motion and joins. Inspect source references individually, in at least two parts when applicable, with timestamp-grounded notes rather than copying a template. Prior reference-review work proceeded in sequential 15–30-second sections; a new full reference-film study needs authorization.
- Still grids are not real-time playback or listening. ASR, correlations and meters are not ear-listening. State what was actually opened, seen, heard and checked, and what could not be checked. Never claim unseen/unheard media was watched/listened.
- Tests/checklists are technical aids, not art approval. Keep technical verification, actual agent review, explicit user approval and publication rights separate. A planning/QC approval does not approve a finished film.
- Preserve rejected and superseded versions and approved outputs with status/hash/provenance. Do not regenerate an approved voice or rewrite approved Escobar to solve another film's problem.
